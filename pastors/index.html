Pastor Mario Alsina

Education, church planting, and mission.

Pastor Mario Alsina has more than three decades of ministry experience in theological education, church planting, pastoral care, and media outreach.

He is founder, rector, and professor at Global Harvest Theological Institute (GHTI), an institution created to offer accessible theological training and prepare servants for the mission of the church.

Pastor Adriana Alsina

Worship, mission, and the next generation.

Adriana Alsina is a music teacher, worship leader, and co-founder of GHTI. Her ministry includes worship formation, church planting, and service to the community in Las Vegas.

With more than three decades of service in worship and church-planting ministries, she also teaches Theology of Worship and brings creativity through children’s books and Christian songs.

What shapes their service

  • Biblical teaching that forms disciples.
  • Worship that points to Christ.
  • Care for families and the local community.
  • Mission that sends people to serve.
